# Claim your Premium key

To qualify: follow [@lexivocabapp](https://x.com/lexivocabapp) and comment
**"key"** on the giveaway post. Then sign in below — the key is issued to
your X account, so nobody can claim yours.

**The first 100 to claim get lifetime Premium.** Everyone after that gets
2 months free.

<p><a class="btn btn-big" href="https://us-central1-lexicon-lab.cloudfunctions.net/xGiveaway/start" rel="nofollow">Sign in with X → get my key</a></p>

Your key appears instantly, with a button that opens Lexi and redeems it —
or redeem by hand in the app: **Settings → Premium → Redeem a Key**.

Not in the giveaway? You can still earn Premium: **every friend you refer
earns you 2 months**, straight from Settings → Premium in the app — and
your first referral unlocks the raccoon companion.

<p class="muted">One key per X account. Signing in only shares your public
profile — we never post, follow, or message on your behalf. Keys expire if
unredeemed; your claim page shows the date, and signing in again re-shows
the same key.</p>
