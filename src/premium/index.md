# Lexi Premium

Premium opens **contrastive training** — the drills that sharpen the line between confusable words, and the constellations they draw in your sky — plus **every companion** for your profile picture.

| | Price | vs. monthly |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly | **$4.99** / month | — |
| Quarterly | **$11.99** / 3 months | saves 20% |
| Annual | **$35.99** / year | saves 40% |

**Subscribe from inside the app:** Settings → Premium. In the United States, checkout opens here in Safari with Apple Pay; everywhere else it goes through the App Store.

Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. Cancel anytime from Settings → Premium in the app — Premium stays on until the end of the period you paid for, and nothing more is charged. Payment for web checkout is handled by [Stripe](https://stripe.com); Lexi never sees your card number.
