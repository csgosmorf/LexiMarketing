# Delete your WordLab account

You can ask us to delete your WordLab account and everything stored for it — every profile,
your learning progress and review schedules, streaks, achievements, leaderboard entries, word
collections, comments, posts and direct messages.

## From the app (fastest)

- **Android:** Settings → Account → **Delete Account & Data** → **Write the Email**, then send the
  email it prepares. It already names your account, so we can find it.
- **iPhone and iPad:** email us from the device (below) and tell us the names of your profiles.

## By email

Write to **lexidevsup@gmail.com** with the subject "Delete my WordLab account" and the names of
your profiles. If you can, send it from the app's Settings as above, so the message includes your
account id.

## What happens next

We confirm by email, then delete all server-side data for the account within **30 days**. Deleting
is permanent: progress can't be restored afterwards. We keep only what the law requires us to
(for example, records of abuse reports). An active Premium subscription is billed by Google Play
or the App Store, so cancel it there as well — deleting your account doesn't stop the store's billing.

To delete just one profile instead, open the app's profile switcher and delete that profile; the
rest of the account stays.
