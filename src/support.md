# WordLab support

**Email [lexidevsup@gmail.com](mailto:lexidevsup@gmail.com)** for anything not answered here: bugs, billing, account or data questions, suggestions. Include your device model and what you were doing when it happened. Inside the app, **Settings → Report a Bug** sends a report with those details attached.

## Progress and devices

**Your progress syncs through iCloud.** Sign in to the same Apple Account in iCloud on each iPhone or iPad and open WordLab; your profiles, learned words, schedules, streaks and collections follow. If a new device starts empty, check that iCloud is signed in (Settings → your name), then reopen the app.

**Profiles** let several people share one Apple Account, each with their own progress. Switch, add or delete them from your name on the home screen.

## Premium and billing

- **Restore a purchase:** open the Premium screen and tap **Restore purchases**.
- **Bought in the app (App Store):** manage or cancel in **Settings → Manage Subscription** inside WordLab, or in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Apple handles refunds at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
- **Bought on the web (United States):** open the Premium screen and tap **Manage billing** to change or cancel, or email us.

## Reminders

WordLab reminds you when words are actually due, at most once a day. Change or turn them off in **Settings → Reminders**, or in your iPhone's Settings → Notifications → WordLab.

## Other players

To report someone or their content, open their profile and use the **…** menu; you can block them from the same place. Blocks are account-wide. **Settings → Blocked Players** lists and undoes them. For urgent abuse, email us.

## A word looks wrong

Use the **⋯** menu on a word (in a puzzle or on its card) to report the clue, definition, example or picture. Every report is read.

## Your data

Deleting a profile (tap your name on the home screen) removes its leaderboard entry, directory listing and friend code. To delete **all** data tied to your account, email [lexidevsup@gmail.com](mailto:lexidevsup@gmail.com) from the device you play on; we delete server-side data within 30 days. Details: [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) · [Terms of Service](/terms/).

## Something isn't working

1. Update WordLab from the App Store.
2. Fully close the app and reopen it; if that doesn't help, restart your device.
3. Still stuck? Email [lexidevsup@gmail.com](mailto:lexidevsup@gmail.com) with your device model, iOS version, and what happened.
